## Basic Waiting Period

Waiting period applies to new customers or those with gaps over 1 year:

- **30 days** for illness and disease cases
- **90 days** for pregnancy complications
- **01 year** for pre-existing conditions and special diseases
- **06 months** for children up to 6 years old: bronchitis, bronchiolitis, pneumonia of all types

## Eligible Participants

1. Insured persons are all Vietnamese citizens or foreigners residing in Vietnam from **15 days old to 65 years old**

2. For children under **6 years old**, insurance is only accepted on the condition that they participate in the same policy with their father and/or mother

3. For children from **15 days old to 3 years old**, inpatient and outpatient medical expense benefits apply according to the prescribed co-payment rate
